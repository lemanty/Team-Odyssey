OUR STORY

Four minds.
One extraordinary journey.

Inspired by an epic.
Created for your everyday.

Every great journey begins with a sense of possibility. Ours begins with a name: Odyssey.

Inspired by Homer’s epic poem, The Odyssey, our name speaks to curiosity, discovery, and the courage to venture beyond the familiar. We bring that spirit to shopping, with a vision of making every visit feel like the beginning of something new.

Founded by four people with different perspectives and a shared ambition, Odyssey was created to offer a fresh way to discover the things you wear, use, and welcome into your home. We believe the best finds do more than fill a wardrobe or a room: they express who you are and bring something meaningful to everyday life.

Our ambition is to become one of the world’s leading shopping brands. That journey starts close to home, with thoughtful choices, personal connections, and an experience designed around you. As we grow, we want to keep the care of a local shop while opening the door to a wider world of possibilities.

Four founders. A shared vision. A new journey in shopping.
Welcome to Odyssey. Your next discovery starts here.
